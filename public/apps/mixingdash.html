KPI 3.5 · Compound delay of the presses

KPI 3.5 = press down time caused by compound delay ÷ (platterns × 24 × 60 × days in the period). The down time comes from the production down time report imported on the Press Downtime Dashboard tab.

Production by compound

  • 3.1 = rework time / total production time x 100. Production time is the sum of Time /min in the daily mixing sheet.
  • 3.2 = production kg / (production minutes / 60). 3.3 = kg of "Re work" batches / production kg x 100. 3.4 = 3.2 / attendance.
  • Weeks follow the Week column in the workbook. Days with no mixing rows are left out.
  • Targets start from the 2026 QMS KPI sheet (3.1 at most 2%, 3.2 at least 2000 kg/h, 3.3 at most 1%, 3.4 at least 250 kg per man hour). Changes are saved per year in this browser. Daily figures swing a lot, so read single days with that in mind.
  • Attendance is not in the workbook, so every day uses the default above (8). Typing a value in the Attendance column sets it for every day in that row. Clear the box to go back to the default.
  • The Production tab uses the description column of daily mixing as the compound, with its compound type and brand. Batches are the QTY column.
